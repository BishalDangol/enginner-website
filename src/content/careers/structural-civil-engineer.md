## Position Scope
Perform 3D structural analysis of telecommunication towers, monopoles, and foundation elements under TIA-222-H, IS 875, and Eurocode standards. Prepare fabrication drafts and multi-tenant colocation loading assessments.

### Requirements & Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's in Civil / Structural Engineering with Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Category A registration.
- 3+ years experience with structural software (tnxTower, STAAD.Pro, ETABS, or OpenTower).
- Proficiency in foundation sizing: pad-and-chimney, rock-anchor post-tensioning, and micro-pile design.
- Familiarity with wind drag calculations, seismic load distributions, and steel member slenderness limits.
