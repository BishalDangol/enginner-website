## Position Scope
Monitor 24/7 telemetry feeds for regional tower obstruction lighting, hybrid solar power systems, and microwave backhaul link status. Coordinate emergency field crew dispatch for rapid incident resolution.

### Requirements & Qualifications
- Diploma or Bachelor's in Electronics & Communication / Telecommunication Engineering.
- Practical experience with Anritsu / Bird site masters for VSWR sweep testing, spectrum analyzers, and OTDR fiber testers.
- Understanding of SNMP monitoring, microwave modulation (QAM), and aviation obstruction light controllers.
- Ability to join on-call rotation for emergency field dispatch within 2 hours.
