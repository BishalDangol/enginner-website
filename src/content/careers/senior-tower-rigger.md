## Position Scope
Lead high-angle tower erection, antenna rigging, and gin-pole assembly teams across national infrastructure sites. You will be responsible for enforcing zero-incident safety protocols and managing lifting gear audits.

### Requirements & Qualifications
- Minimum 4 years verifiable experience working at heights on guyed masts and lattice towers (up to 100m).
- Valid Working-at-Height Certification (IRATA, SPRAT, or OSHA 1926 Subpart M equivalent).
- Proven competency in gin-pole rigging, cable hoisting, capstan winch operations, and antenna mechanical azimuth/tilt alignment.
- Complete familiarity with double-lanyard 100% tie-off rules, full-body harnesses, and fall arresters.
