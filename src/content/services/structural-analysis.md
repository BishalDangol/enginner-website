## Structural Integrity Audits for Multi-Tenant Colocation

Before adding 5G Massive MIMO active antenna units or large 1.8m microwave dishes to existing masts, operators must prove remaining structural capacity. Apex conducts rigorous non-destructive field audits and 3D finite element simulation to certify structural stability.

### Engineering Analysis Workflow
1. **As-Built Physical Mapping:** Ultrasonic steel thickness measurement, member cross-section gauge logging, and drone photogrammetric 3D point cloud generation.
2. **FEA Modeling & Load Calculations:** Wind pressure and gust factor modeling incorporating radial ice accretion, appurtenance drag coefficients, and torsional deflection limits.
3. **Capacity Rating Reporting:** Deliver detailed member-by-member stress ratios (P/Pc, M/Mc) indicating exact percentage of allowable capacity utilized.
4. **Structural Reinforcement Designs:** Bolted split-pipe member sleeving, diagonal bracing additions, and torque-arm stabilizer retrofits for tower strengthening without decommissioning.
