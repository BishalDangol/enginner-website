## Long-Haul & Sub-Gigahertz Microwave Links

When mountainous topography renders optical fiber trenching economically or physically impractical, high-availability microwave links provide essential backhaul bandwidth with 99.999% carrier availability.

### Engineering & Rigging Capabilities
- **Path Profile & Fresnel Zone Calculation:** LiDAR terrain elevation analysis with clearance modeling for atmospheric k-factor refraction (k = 4/3 nominal, down to k = 0.6 extreme multipath).
- **Sub-Band Frequency Bands:** 6 GHz, 7/8 GHz (long-haul backbone spans up to 65 km), 11 GHz to 23 GHz (metro backhaul), and 70/80 GHz E-Band (ultra-high 10 Gbps capacity).
- **Dual-Polarized Dish Rigging:** Optical sighting and mechanical micro-tilt alignment with receive signal level (RSL) measured within 1 dB of theoretical link budget calculations.
- **Feedline & Waveguide Sweep Testing:** Return loss and Voltage Standing Wave Ratio (VSWR < 1.15) testing across full operating frequency bands.
