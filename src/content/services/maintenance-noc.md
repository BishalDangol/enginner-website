## Continuous Infrastructure Uptime & Emergency Rigging

Telecom infrastructure demands active preventive maintenance to survive lightning strikes, monsoons, and extreme thermal cycling. Apex maintains dedicated field service trucks equipped with calibrated test sets and rigging winches across regional depots.

### Preventive Maintenance Checklist (Every 180 Days)
- **Fastener Torque Re-Check:** Re-torquing 100% of leg flange bolts and anchor nuts to certified values with paint marking.
- **Guy Wire Tensioning:** Dynamometer tension check on guyed masts adjusted for ambient steel temperature.
- **Grounding Grid Inspection:** 3-point fall-of-potential earth resistance testing ensuring ground impedance stays strictly below 5.0 Ohms.
- **Aviation Warning System:** Solar/battery telemetry checks, photocell verification, and dual LED strobe flash test conforming to ICAO Annex 14 requirements.
- **Corrosion Remediation:** Mechanical wire-brush de-rusting, cold galvanizing zinc compound application (> 95% zinc dust), and aviation orange/white polyurethane painting.
