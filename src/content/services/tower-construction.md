## Scope of Civil & Structural Rigging

Apex Tower & Infrastructure delivers end-to-end tower engineering from greenfield geotechnical borings to final gin-pole assembly. Every tower section is constructed using ASTM A572 Grade 50 high-tensile structural steel, hot-dip galvanized in accordance with ASTM A123 to provide corrosion endurance in alpine condensation and monsoon humidity.

### Structural Typologies Deployed
- **4-Legged Angular Lattice Towers (18m–80m):** High-rigidity structures engineered for high antenna load density (up to 12 sector antennas + 4 solid parabolic dishes) with torsional deflection under 0.5 degrees at 160 km/h wind velocity.
- **Guyed Mast Systems (45m–120m):** Cost-effective, high-elevation towers for mountain ridge relays, featuring prestressed structural strand guys, turnbuckles with safety lock-wires, and concrete ground anchors.
- **Polygonal Flanged Monopoles (15m–40m):** Compact footprint installations for dense urban roadsides and enterprise campuses, built with internal cable ladder routing.
- **Rooftop Stubs & Ballasted Grillages (6m–18m):** Non-penetrating ballasted frames and structural slab tie-ins designed to avoid membrane punctures.

### Field Verification Standards
1. Full bolt-torque audit using calibrated torque wrenches (ASTM A325 fasteners).
2. Theodolite verticality alignment: max vertical deflection < H/1000.
3. Galvanization coating thickness testing: magnetic thickness gauge verification (> 86 µm).
