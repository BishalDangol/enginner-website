## Carrier-Grade Optical Transport Networks

Our fiber teams deploy long-distance optical backbone and metro distribution networks with low optical attenuation (< 0.20 dB/km at 1550 nm) for telecom operators and wholesale broadband networks.

### Deployment Methodologies
- **All-Dielectric Self-Supporting (ADSS) Fiber:** Tensioned stringing across power transmission rights-of-way with corona coil vibration dampers and dielectric pole hardware.
- **Micro-Duct HDD & Open Trenching:** High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) sub-duct jetting with pneumatic cable blowing for armored underground backbone loops.
- **Fusion Splicing & Optical Time-Domain Reflectometry (OTDR):** Core-alignment fusion splicing with splice loss certified below 0.03 dB per joint. Bi-directional OTDR event trace reports delivered for every strand.
