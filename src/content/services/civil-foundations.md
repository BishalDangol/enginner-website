## Geotechnical Engineering & Foundation Design

Infrastructure durability begins below the surface. In seismic zones and steep mountainous terrain, our in-house geotechnical engineering team conducts Standard Penetration Tests (SPT), rock core drilling, and slope slip-circle stability simulations prior to excavation.

### Foundation Typologies
- **Pad & Chimney Concrete Foundations:** Heavy reinforced concrete bases designed to resist high overturning moments, uplift forces, and horizontal shear under TIA-222-H Class IV wind loads.
- **Micro-Piled & Rock-Anchored Footings:** High-capacity post-tensioned Dywidag bar anchors drilled 6m to 12m into solid bedrock for exposed ridge-line installations where heavy cement transport is constrained.
- **Raft & Mat Foundations:** Distributed load slabs deployed in soft alluvial soils and terai basins with high water tables.
- **Retaining Walls & Gabion Slope Protection:** Geotextile-reinforced stepped gabion revetments preventing monsoon slope creep around compound perimeters.

### Concrete Quality Assurance
- Ready-Mix & Field Batch Testing: M25 / M30 minimum design strength.
- Slump testing on every batch pour, plus 7-day and 28-day cylinder compressive strength crushing tests.
- Rebar placement verification complying with seismic detailing guidelines (IS 13920 / ACI 318).
