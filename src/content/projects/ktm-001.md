## Project Overview

High-altitude relay installation on the southwest rim of Kathmandu Valley at 2,551 meters elevation. The site provides vital microwave Line-of-Sight transmission bridging the central valley telecom ring with the southern Terai corridor.

### Structural Engineering Constraints
- **Extreme Wind Loading:** Designed for 180 km/h wind gusts (TIA-222-H Class V) due to high exposure at mountain crest.
- **Geotechnical Rock Anchors:** Due to thin overburden soil, foundation anchors utilized 32mm post-tensioned high-yield rock bolts drilled 8 meters directly into fractured gneiss bedrock with non-shrink structural grout.
- **Guy Wire Configuration:** 3-way guy configuration with 3 elevation levels (18m, 36m, 54m) utilizing 1x7 bridge strand galvanized guy wires with vibration dampers.
- **Lightning Protection:** Copper air terminals with dual 70 mm² down-conductors terminated into a radial earthing ring with bentonite soil conditioning, achieving 2.8 Ohms earth resistance.

### Outcomes
- Site completed on schedule within 28 field days with zero safety incidents.
- Now carrying 4x 1.2m solid parabolic microwave antennas and 6x 4G/5G sector antennas.
