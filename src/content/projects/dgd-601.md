## Project Overview

A robust 30-meter monopole deployed at the junction of the East-West Highway and Mahakali corridor, expanding mobile broadband coverage into regional trading hubs.

### Structural Engineering Constraints
- **High-Yield Tubular Steel:** Multi-sided tapered polygonal pole manufactured from Q345B steel with factory-applied internal rust inhibitor.
- **Pre-Engineered Foundation Cage:** Circular anchor bolt cage with 24x high-strength M36 anchor rods embedded into an M30 reinforced concrete spread footing.
- **Fast-Track Rigging:** Mechanical crane erection executed within a single 8-hour road-closure window.
