## Project Overview

A heavy-duty 4-legged self-supporting lattice tower established at Sarangkot (1,600m ASL) providing primary telecommunications coverage over Pokhara Valley and microwave links into Annapurna foothill districts.

### Structural Engineering Constraints
- **Multi-Tenant Colocation Design:** Engineered for 3 independent mobile operators, supporting up to 18 panel antennas and 6 microwave backhaul dishes with an effective projected area (EPA) of 14.5 m².
- **Pad & Chimney Foundation:** Reinforced concrete raft with 4 pedestal chimneys (140 m³ total concrete pour), reinforced with high-yield deformed bars resisting overturning moments under seismic zone IV conditions.
- **Aviation Warning System:** Medium-intensity red obstacle light (ICAO Type B) with battery backup and solar recharging system.

### Outcomes
- Operational handover certified by national telecom regulators.
- Over 99.99% link availability maintained through winter storms.
