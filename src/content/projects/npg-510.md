## Project Overview

An 80-meter tall guyed lattice mast engineered for high-elevation microwave transmission spanning the Karnali river basin. The mast acts as the primary junction node for western telecom backhaul.

### Structural Engineering Constraints
- **Multi-Level Guying System:** 4-tier guy system with stay wires anchored to heavy gravity reinforced concrete anchor blocks buried 3 meters below grade.
- **Torsional Stabilizer Star Frames:** Anti-rotation star torque brackets installed at the top microwave dish tier to prevent signal misalignment during severe thunderstorms.
- **Aircraft Safety Compliance:** Dual synchronous solar-powered LED strobes with automatic day/night twilight switching.
