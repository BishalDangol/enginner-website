## Project Overview

A high-load square lattice steel structure positioned near the southern international optical gateway. This installation carries cross-border DWDM optical amplifier nodes and dense microwave carrier links.

### Structural Engineering Constraints
- **Deep Mat Foundation with Geotextile Stabilization:** High water table (1.5m below surface) during monsoon required continuous dewatering, 200mm stone soling with geotextile separation, and waterproofed sulfate-resistant cement concrete.
- **Corrosion Barrier Coating:** Polyurethane topcoat over hot-dip galvanizing to withstand industrial sulfur fumes from neighboring manufacturing plants.
- **Lightning Earth System:** Deep chemical earth electrodes driven to 12 meters reaching perpetual ground moisture, measuring 1.2 Ohms.
