## Project Overview

A compact-footprint tubular steel monopole designed for rapid deployment along the Narayangadh–Bharatpur commercial arterial road where land area was limited to a 6x6 meter compound.

### Structural Engineering Constraints
- **Compact Bored Cast-in-Situ Pile Foundation:** 1.2m diameter single reinforced concrete drilled shaft extending 14 meters into sandy gravel strata to resist high bending moment without sprawling footings.
- **Flanged Slip-Joint Assembly:** 3-section polygonal steel shaft with internal ladder, fall-arrest cable system, and internal RF feeder cable routing minimizing visual clutter.
- **Wind Speed Certification:** 160 km/h basic design wind speed with less than 1.0 degree deflection at the mast head.

### Outcomes
- Turnkey civil and erection completed within 14 working days.
- Fully integrated with smart meter power monitoring and fiber termination panel.
